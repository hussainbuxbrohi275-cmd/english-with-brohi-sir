About the Teacher

Meet Saif Jan Brohi.

I am Saif Jan Brohi, an English learner and teacher who wants to make English Grammar and Spoken English easier for students. My teaching approach focuses on simple explanations, practical examples, exercises and speaking practice — so that English feels learnable, not intimidating.

Help students understand grammar easily, grow their vocabulary, and speak English with confidence.

Not perfect English. Confident English. The kind that gets used in the classroom, in exams, and in everyday conversation without second-guessing every word.

Our Method

How we actually teach — the English WITH BROHI Sir approach.

🧩

Diagnose first

Every student starts with a short assessment so we know their exact grammar gaps and comfort level speaking — before we teach a single lesson.

✍️

Correct in the moment

Mistakes get fixed as they happen — in speech and in writing — with the reasoning explained, not just the correction.

🗣️

Practice out loud, every class

Every single session includes speaking time. Confidence is built through repetition, not a single "spoken English" unit once a term.

📊

Track and share progress

Parents get a monthly note on what's improved and what we're still working on — no surprises at report card time.
